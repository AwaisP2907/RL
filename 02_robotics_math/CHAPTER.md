# Chapter 2 — Robotics Math

**Dates:** Nov 30–Dec 3

## Objective

Understand rotations, homogeneous transforms, forward kinematics and numerical inverse kinematics.

## Gate

- Do FK and numerical IK for a 2-link arm, by hand and in code.

## Plan

### Topics

- Linear transformations, matrix multiplication, change of basis
- Rotation matrices and homogeneous transforms
- Forward kinematics, velocity kinematics (Jacobian), numerical inverse kinematics

### Resources

- [3Blue1Brown — Essence of Linear Algebra](https://www.3blue1brown.com/topics/linear-algebra) (Ch 3, 4, 13)
- [Modern Robotics videos](https://modernrobotics.northwestern.edu/nu-gm-book-resource/) (Ch 3.1–3.3, Ch 4, Ch 5.1, Ch 6.2) · [book wiki](https://hades.mech.northwestern.edu/index.php/Modern_Robotics)

### Build

- `transforms.py`: rotation and homogeneous transform helpers.
- `two_link_arm.py`: FK and numerical IK for a 2-link arm.
